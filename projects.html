Past Projects

A few of the systems teams designed, launched, and evaluated in past semesters. Enrolled students can see the full posters from past semesters on Canvas.

  • EchoBreaker Fall 2025

    Reduce political polarization by showing people how their beliefs about the other party compare with what members of that party actually think.

  • CopeClippy Winter 2026

    Curb hostile replies on Reddit by prompting people to reflect on their emotions before they respond to a post that upsets them.

  • Social Mirror Winter 2026

    Help people break out of doomscrolling on Twitter by steering their sessions toward what they came to see.

  • ClearFrame Fall 2025

    Bring local perspectives to international news by showing how outlets from the region cover the story a reader is already reading.

EECS 480 / CSE 598, University of Michigan, Winter 2027. Instructor: Farnaz Jahanbakhsh.
